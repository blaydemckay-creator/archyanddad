🎮 Dad vs Archy — Tank Duel

Two players, one keyboard. Take turns aiming your tank, mind the wind, and lob a shell over the hill. First to 3 hits wins — no prizes, just bragging rights!

Keyboard: ← → aim · ↑ ↓ power · SPACE fire · B = change weapon (Normal · 💣 Bouncy · ✸ Cluster · ☢️ Nuke · 🔫 MG). Players take turns on the same keyboard.

How to play

It's a friendly artillery duel. On your turn, set your angle and power, check the wind at the top, and fire over the hill. Land a shell on the other tank to score. First to 3 hits takes the match.

Because it's all about aim and not reflexes, it's a fair fight whether you're a little tacker or the old man. Perfect for a quick round at the kitchen table. 🎮
