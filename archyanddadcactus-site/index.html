How to play

  1. Look how far away Dad is standing.
  2. Hold the space bar to charge your kick.
  3. Release to land the ball in his arms.
  4. Every perfect pass = +1 streak. One miss and it's over!
  5. 🌬️ After 4 in a row, wind kicks in — compensate for it!

Why we do it

It started with a single packet of seeds on the kitchen table and a curious kid asking "Dad, is it bigger today?" every morning. That ten seconds each day — leaning over a pot together, wondering at something alive — turned into the whole point.

So we bottled it. Every Archy & Dad kit is built to be made together: simple enough for little hands, satisfying enough that grown-ups get hooked too.
